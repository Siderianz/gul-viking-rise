# Testing Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing changes.

## Core Flow

- Open `index.html`.
- Confirm the language gate is visible after refreshing the home page.
- Click the Russian rune and confirm the site opens in Russian.
- Refresh home, then click the English rune and confirm the site opens in English.
- Use the RU/EN switcher in the header on an inner page.

## Navigation

- Check every top navigation link:
  - Home
  - Migration request
  - Achievements
  - Media
  - Guides
  - Kingdom structure
  - Contacts and links
- Confirm the active menu item matches the current page.

## Guides

- Open `guides.html`.
- Confirm guide tabs render.
- Confirm search filters tabs/content.
- Confirm the Google Sheet and PvP calculator links open in a new tab.
- Check that the guide interface language follows the selected site language.

## Kingdom

- Open `kingdom.html`.
- Confirm The Punisher is R5.
- Confirm THOR HUSKY, RocketStar, Winter Sandor, SuRTuR, береза, HellKatz, and JaneFoster are R4.
- Confirm officer avatars load.

## Accessibility

- Navigate with `Tab` and confirm focus is visible.
- Confirm language buttons are reachable by keyboard.
- Confirm forms can be focused and typed into.

## Responsive Widths

- 360 px
- 390 px
- 768 px
- 1024 px
- 1440 px

Check the header, language gate, officer cards, guide tabs, and guide tables at each width.
